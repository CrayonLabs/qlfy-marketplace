# VC: founders

## Before the first search

- Call `overview` with `purpose: invest`. Put the sector ("climate tech"), the place and "founder" in the brief as requirements; past exits as a requirement only when the user asks for them.
- The overview's count can be small: founders with an exit in one city and sector are few. Say so, and offer to widen the place or the sector rather than dropping the founder or exit requirement silently.

## Questions after the overview

Ask only the ones the brief leaves open (core-workflow, "After the overview"):

- **Stage:** pre-seed and seed, Series A, or later.
- **Sector,** when the brief is broad: name two or three likely ones as options.
- **Track record:** any founder, repeat founders only, or founders with an exit.
- **Place:** the city or country only, or wider.

## What fit means

- A founder or co-founder of the company they work at now, in the sector.
- A track record: a previous company founded, ideally with an exit (acquired or public).
- An early company now: founded recently, small team.

## Default claims

- "Has founded at least two companies"
- "Previously founded a company that was acquired or went public"
- "Currently founded or is building a company working on <sector>"
- "Their current company was founded within the last 3 years"

## The list

- Name, current company and role, then the evidence for each checked claim: the companies they founded and what happened to them, quoted from the reasons.
- Keep people with `not_enough_information` on the exit claim; profiles often don't say how a company ended.
- Offer `find_contact` only for the founders the user wants to meet.
- Save the people the user picks with `shortlist_candidate` (a short note on why), so the list survives the conversation and comes back with `get_search`.
